‘lo there everyone. I’ve been thinking of some ways to get the community more
involved with one another and I decided to introduce TESLORE Challenges. Held
once a week every Thursday, TCs will basically challenge members of our
community to write something lorey about something mundane or mysterious from
the games as a sort of writing competition. It can be about a simple object, to
an abandoned settlement, to a NPC that just seems a bit weird (but in a good
way).

Your stories can be as long as you want them to be, and be about what you want
them to be about as long as they are focusing on the item in question. On Sunday
during The Weekly Community Thread the winner will be announced (depending on
the most well-liked story) and will receive...something...special.

Just one rule everyone:

DO NOT DOWNVOTE THESE STORIES. That way, everyone can look at and evaluate the
stories equally (its also being cowardly and cheating).

\[Actually, perhaps we shall do a Google Forms Poll/Survey instead. I’ll work on
that.\]

But let’s get started, shall we?

The Item of the Week

http://www.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Unused_NPCs#Spirit_of_the_Ancient_Traveler

The Spirit of the Ancient Traveler is an Unused Nonplayable Character in Skyrim.
Who is he/her? How old are they? And why is Wayward Pass sacred to them?

Well, you folks know what to do.

Write about it. Good Luck everyone.
