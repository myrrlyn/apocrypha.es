- To: First Emissary Elenwen Jororin, Thalmor Ambassador to Skyrim
- Morndas, 2nd of Sun’s Dusk, 4E 20X

Ambassador Elenwen Jororin,

You are hereby ordered to prepare the Embassy of Skyrim for guests. The
leadership council of Alinor and I are significantly disturbed by the rumors
coming out of Skyrim and your lack of progress.

I will arrive in Skyrim in two months time at which I will conduct a formal
review of all active Dominion operations in Skyrim as part of your yearly
evaluation.

We are through waiting.

Sincerely,

Vindicator-General Aicantar Arana
