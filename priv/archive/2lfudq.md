To: Alinor Council

CC: First Emissary Elenwen Jororin, Thalmor Ambassador to Skyrim

Turdas, the 3rd of Evening Star, 4E 20X

Council,

Upon my arrival in Skyrim, it has become readily apparent as to why Skyrim has
been slipping from our grasp. A formal review of all active Dominion operations
in Skyrim has confirmed a significant lack of progress in the province. In the
last several months, the Thalmor unit deployed to Skyrim has lost at least seven
agents. In addition, the embassy itself was breached by an individual we can
only assume to the be the Dragonborn. Thus, since the First Emissary here has
proven far too incompetent to run an embassy, let alone maintain our presence in
Skyrim, by the powers invested in me by Internal Affairs, I hereby assume
command of our Skyrim embassy. Elenwen Jororin will return to Alinor for
reeducation. Per our discussion, all Skyrim personnel are hereby ordered to
renew their investigations into the various factions of Skyrim. I believe we can
secure Skyrim before it is lost to us completely.

Sincerely,

Vindicator-General Aicantar Arana
