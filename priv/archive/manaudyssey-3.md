**At dawn the _Tesaloricus_ sails from the Imperial City Isle, whose true name
is lost but can be still heard if one really thinks about it, and enters the
Zephyr Rumare. Dathfliin turns to stare at his awestruck crew and smiles.**

“What lies beneath us, is also above us. We shall follow this stream of air
until we reach the point where it empties into the Niben Squall. And once there,
we shall leave this land.”

**And the crew gave a hearted cheer of agreement, and the _Tesaloricus_ sailed
through the Cloud Kingdom. And it was then that Dathfliin felt the eyes of
someone on his back, and turned around to notice the most beautiful woman he had
ever seen floating in the air.**

“A breeze-daughter, perhaps a child of Mother Kynareth? If you truly are an
Aurae, please do not inform her of our presence here, for our quest is dire and
must not be hindered, especially by her.”

**The spirit smiled, and then became more clearer and vivid before the First
Captain. Her skin was light as caramel and glowed with supernatural light. Her
hair itself seemed to be made from songs. She smiled, and Dathfliin became
half-blind from her radiance. In the center of her bosom lied a sundered red
star, and within it one could see the entirety of Tamriel in full.**

“Calm yourself, son of Cyrod, for I am your mother even though I did not birth
you. I am Al-Esh, Paravant, Perrif, Paravania, and Aleshut. Morihaus was my
Lover and Star-Made my Knight.”

**Dathfliin gasps in horror and bows at the spirit’s feet, trembling.**

“Alessia! Mother of the Land, I bow to you and I pray that you forget my
blasphemies for mistaking you.”

**The Spirit of Alessia places her hand on Dathfliin’s shoulder and exhales, and
her breath washes over the lad and stills him.**

“You are forgiven even though you did no wrong. For I have come to advise you,
as I have always done for all of my people. Do not look for your crew; this
vision is not meant for their ears but yours alone, but when you act upon it
they will follow you without hesitation.

You wish to sail the Sixteen Seas of the Void, which was chartered by our former
masters before they driven out of the Heartlands. You do this out of love for
your lost father, which I favor, and so I have given you safe passage.

But there are Eight others that you must give you leave, for they also hold
dominion of these lands that I have become. Why do you think there are Nine
Realms of Dawn’s Beauty? Is it coincidence that there is also Nine Divines? No,
there isn’t. History is destined to repeat; the echoes can still be heard. If
you truly wish to leave Nirn’s embrace, let her Tenders give you the keys to her
door.”

**Dathfliin nods, but also looks perplexed.**

“If I may ask of you, High Alessia, what about the lands outside of Tamriel? Are
we to visit them as well?”

**The Spirit of Alessia gently shakes her head.**

“And what about them? Those lands should be kept outside of your thoughts, as
they are not needed. When Nirn gave birth to Tamriel they were second-placed and
are embittered, like a flower that does not receive enough rain. Speaking to
their Rulers would only curse your vessel, damning you and its crew to the cruel
mercies of Oblivion. The Eight Rulers, however, know you are coming and will
come find you.”

**Dathfliin nods again. The _Tesaloricus_ rounds a bend in the Zephyr and can
now see into the distance, where they see the White-Gold of the clouds and it
looks wonderful. He turns back to the Spirit of Alessia.**

“Who are these Eight Rulers? And where can I find them?”

**Alessia spreads her arms and an image unravels in the wind between them,
showing beautiful scenes of the land. As she talks, the image changes as if a
master painter is etching them out of thin air.**

“The Eight Rulers reign after me, for I am the First Ruler even though they have
ruled far longer than I have. They reign in the Lands outside of Cyrodiil, and
like us are tied to this Starry Heart, like how a Harp needs its strings.

The Second Ruler is Man-Child of Akatosh, and presides in the Land of Snow,
where his breath brings forth winter and the keeping of it permits spring. Speak
to him, and he will give you the Key of Voice.

The Third Ruler is Only-Needed-At-The-Moment, and walks absently in the Land of
Red Sand, where he leads but doesn’t allow his people to follow unless they
truly care. Speak to him, and he will give you the Key of Force.

The Fourth Ruler is Hidden-Exiled, and she represents a life once lost. In the
Land of History she resides, and she resides there alone and I feel sorry for
her. Speak to her, and she will give you the Key of Civilly.

The Fifth Ruler is First-King, and his ghost still roars away the dangers of the
world from his kingdom. From the Land of Wood he hails, and that is where his
spirit will continue to prowl. Speak to him, and he will give you the Key of
Change.

The Sixth Ruler is Braided-Third, and he is the closest to the Void than anyone
else, even though he doesn’t reside in it. In the Land of Sugar you will be able
to find him, a single person wearing the appearance of many. Speak to him, and
he will give you the Key of Lunacy.

The Seventh Ruler is Three-But-Not-One, and they argue in the Land of Ash where
the land bleeds god blood. The First might try to harm you, and the Second will
most certainly ignore you, but the Third will probably do both. Speak to them,
and they will give you the Key of Love.

The Eighth Ruler is Many-Minds, and is more than one person at a time always.
Rooted firmly in the Land of Mud, they alter their world to best shape it to the
one that they lost. Speak to them, and they will give you the Key of Growth.

The Ninth Ruler is Stopped-In-Motion, and is no longer here but the cries of his
people make him stay. Isolated in the Land of Culture he is, and he wishes that
he could just escape his bonds. Maybe one day he will. Speak to him, and he will
give you the Key of Time.

Once you attain these Eight Keys, only then will you be able to unlock the
Nirvana Pillars, which were sundered in times lost and protected by the First
Knight of this world. And after that, the Void Seas will be opened to you.”

**Dathfliin understood the wisdom of the Spirit of Alessia, even though she
talked in riddles.**

“And what of your Key, majesty? What is it?”

**Those words had barely left Dathfliin’s mouth when he head the sound of a
bird’s beautiful melody, and turned to find the source of its song. From the
heaven’s a winged creature made entirely out of blinding light descended from
the hidden peak of the Sky White-Gold and flies into the figurehead of the ship,
a carving of a beautiful woman. After the light fades away, a crown rests upon
the woman’s head, glowing red.**

“My Key is the Key of Liberty. Wherever you go, the Lords of Misrule will know
not to try to keep you, for you belong to another world that is your home. That
is my gift to you, Dathfliin. Use it well.”

**The Spirit of Alessia begins to fade away, rejoining in her union with the
land.**

“I have persuaded the Auraes of the Sky Kingdom along with their husbands to
help speed you to the Land of Snow. But they can only do this once, for Kynareth
has grown suspicious, and she is your greatest enemy. Go now in peace,
Dathfliin. Love guide you.”

**With those words the Spirit of Alessia completely fades away, and new souls
materialize in the sky. Women made from thermals wearing dresses of fog arrive
on the back of frozen owls, being steered by bird-headed men. Latching coils of
smoke wisps around the _Tesaloricus_, one of the men with the head of a parrot
turns to face the astounded crew and screeches. The Night Hawk turns to face the
First Captain, as he is the only one present capable of understanding Bird
Talk.**

“Hang on to something, he says! For they are the Blistering Winds of Winter, and
it is in their nature to not worry about life all that much!”

**Dathfliin smiles, for he knows this is not the last time he will be glad that
he picked a crew such as this. Each of them held talents that would be
beneficial for this journey, and he ordered all of the crew beneath deck as the
Blistering Winds of Winter flew the _Tesaloricus_ across the heavens. For the
first time ever snow fell during the summer as the Sky Children carried the
Voidship across plains and lakes and mountains until they were over ice. With
surprising gentleness the wind spirits docked the ship on a glacier attached to
the northernmost shore, and were gone without another word.**

**The First Captain and his crew climbed back to the deck (except poor Maering,
who would’ve destroyed the ice block they all stood upon, but his friend Sudon
the Withstanding stayed down below to keep him company). Anshar scratches the
back of his neck, and called down a sliver of a star so they could see in the
fading light.**

“First Captain, shall we set out from the ship? Or at least, drag her further
ashore so we don’t drift all the way back to the Old Land?”

**Dathfliin raises his eyebrow at the question and turns to the distance.**

“We will not drift into the Old Land, for that is one of the places we are not
allowed to go. As I told you all below, the Second Ruler will come and speak to
us when he wills it. We must not seem impatient, or he could ruin us.”

**The deck grows quiet as the crew continues to wait for the First Ruler, with
no one keeping track of how much time is passing. But suddenly they all hear the
sound of wings beating, and with a earth-shattering noise a great wyrm made of
swirling frost alights in front of the _Tesaloricus_. Its scales shine with an
impossible cleanliness, and his eyes glow like the moons. From his feet a wave
of rime crawls across the ice and towards the ship, but Dica the Cold,
Iizyoldein, Freurn, and Hollan Mark use their powers to keep it at bay. But
there is fear in their eyes, and Dica speaks for all.**

“Dathfliin, we cannot hold this enchanted ice back much longer! If it reaches
the ship, we will all freeze solid and nothing will be able to melt us for we
are so far north. Maybe if we fight–”

**The wyrm turns around, and with one look silences the man. He then turns back
to face the First Captain, looking almost amused.**

“So you believe you can fight me? Do you not who I am? My voice spreads
throughout all the known world and ices it over. But am I right that you know
who I am, Joor? Come to me from your vessel, he who is known as Dathfliin, so we
may speak.”

**At those words the crew of the _Tesaloricus_ drew their arms, shielding their
Captain. Nev Solitha turns to face Dathfliin.**

“Sir, get below deck while we protect you. We are familiar with dragon-kin, and
while killing this one will not be easy we will do it to protect thee.”

**Dathfliin calmly shakes his head, smiling.**

“No, for this is the Second Ruler that Saint Alessia spoke of. To enter the Void
we must first gain the Key of Voice, which he keeps. The blessing of Alessia
will protect me.”

**With those words, Dathfliin jumps off the deck and lands on the ice block in
the middle of the ocean, walking timidly with courage towards the Second Ruler,
who watches him and speaks.**

“Tidings to you, Dathfliin Grieves. I look at you and see a mortal that has his
mind made up and ready to act. I am Ysmir, Dragon of the North, and Second Ruler
of this land. You have come for the Key of Voice, have you not?”

**Dathfliin, speechless, nods his head and Ysmir laughs.**

“Do not be afraid, Dathfliin. For I know that you search for your father and I
hope you find him. I have no qualms against giving you the Key of Voice, but I
must first know that you will use it wisely. So I will ask of you to answer this
riddle, and if you answer correctly my Key is yours: Why do you have the
privilege to speak?”

**The First Captain stands there, thinking over the question for several hours
before turning to stare directly into the eyes of Ysmir and looks determined.**

“For the same reason we have mouths.”

**Ysmir nods his head gently and smiles, and then turns to look at the
figurehead of the _Tesaloricus_ along with Dathfliin. Filling his mouth with the
entirety of winter, the Second Ruler roars a blast of frost at the figurehead
for several minutes before stopping. The figurehead is now holding a war horn in
her left hand glowing white like snow.**

“I yield you my Key for you to use, Dathfliin Grieves. My Key is the Key of
Voice. Wherever you go, the Lords of Turmoil will be forced to listen to what
you have to say. That is my gift to you, Dathfliin Grieves. Use it well. Now be
gone from this land, for your journey is not yet completed.”

**With those words Ysmir flapped his great wings and the glacier that the
_Tesaloricus_ rested upon was pushed away from the Land of Snow and floated
south and west at incredible speeds. The crew didn’t have enough time to go
below deck, and watched as the glacier began to melt as the temperature grew
drier and hotter before turning into water completely, and the _Tesaloricus_
found themselves in the Land of Red Sand, completely moored in the hot dunes.**

**It was Ner, Warrior-King of Adac, that reacted first amongst the others. He
drew his sword, and prepared to swing that upon ropes to cut upon the sand when
Dathfliin grabbed his arm.**

“Do you know what you were about to do, my friend? This is the Land of the Third
Ruler and striking it will not only harm him but enrage him in many ways. His is
unstoppable once he gets going, and unkillable once he stops. Let us wait, and
see what happens.”

**Ner nodded, but looked disturbed.**

“I will do as you say, my Captain, but if we do not get out of this heat soon
the wood of our boat shall catch aflame.”

**Dathfliin nodded, and ordered the crew to their posts.**

“Which is why we shall leave as if we are only passing through, and find the
Third Ruler unless he find’s us. Ready the rows, and prepare to set course!”

**With mighty arms the heroes of the _Tesaloricus_ struggled through sandy sea
when all of a sudden there was a groan and the crew held on to their posts as
the sand shifted beneath the boat and began to lift them into the sky, taking on
the form of a hand. As it lifted them higher and higher, Dathfliin was able to
see the sand transform into that of man who wore many faces.**

“A boat sailing in the sand, a strange sight to be seen but also an admirable
one. I wish to speak to only your captain, the mortal known as Dathfliin, before
I let you go.”

**The Third Ruler grabbed Dathfliin from the boat by his collar and lifted him
into the towards his faces while holding the ship in his hand like a child’s
trinket.**

“So you are Dathfliin Grieves, who wishes to rescue his father from the Realms
of the Lords of Misrule without their knowledge. I am the HoonDing, Third Ruler
of this Land, and holder of the Key of Force. Like the one before and those that
will come after I ask of you to answer a question of mine. Do you accept or
not?”

**Dathfliin nodded his head.**

“I give you my word and my honor to answer truthfully, for I accept.”

**The HoonDing smiled with all of his faces.**

“Good. Here is what I ask of you: What is the greatest strength a mortal can
wield but a god cannot, but a god working through a mortal can?”

**The Captain of the _Tesaloricus_ furrowed his brow trying to discern the
answer, when he looked towards the setting sun and knew what he should say.**

“Strength against adversity, the act of standing despite people leaving you no
legs to stand upon and giving no matter to the fate that may befall you.”

**The HoonDing smiled again, and placed Dathfliin gently back down in his ship,
which he sets within the sand. With a bellow the Third Ruler summons a sandstorm
that strikes the prow, and when it subsides there is now holding a sword in her
right hand that glows a blood red.**

“I yield you my Key for you to use, Dathfliin Grieves. My Key is the Key of
Force. Wherever you go, there will always be a way to escape from the Lords of
Turmoil. That is my gift to you, Dathfliin Grieves. Use it well. Now be gone
from this land, for your journey is not yet completed.”

**With a sigh the HoonDing collapsed into the sand, and the strength of it
falling into the ground caused the _Tesaloricus_ to be flung through across the
land and slightly towards the north, where they landed in the blue sea of the
province of the Fourth Ruler.**

**Dathfliin heard his crew sigh in relief because of the cool ocean breeze, and
saw Viscountess N lean down towards the sea to speak with her husband, but
frowned when he saw her frown.**

“What is it, Wife of All Oceans? What makes you frown like so? Does the water
inform you of foe we must dispose of?”

**The Viscountess N pointed to an island near where they had landed.**

“Yes, they do, but it is no ordinary foe. For the Fourth Ruler is coming to us,
but her mood is one of cruel intent and not of friendship.”

**Dathfliin yelled at his crew to take up arms, and he himself drew You as he
turned to the west and gasped to see a ship of silver sleek his eyes had never
seen before gracing the air towards him. What startled him the most, however,
was that the craft began to speak in a female’s voice.**

“I have heard of what you are attempting to do, Dathfliin Grieves, and I grow
weary about it! What right do you have to my Key?”

**The Captain slowly signaled his companions to lower their arms, to not anger
the craft anymore than it already was.**

“Am I right in saying that you are the Fourth Ruler, leader of this land?”

**The craft chuckled, and shined a beam of light upon the deck to observe the
crew and the one that spoke.**

“You are indeed right Dathfliin Grieves, for I know it is you onboard that ship
that has spoken. I have sensed that my fellows Rulers before me have given you
their keys, but I do not trust this power in the hands of a mortal.”

**The Captain kneeled before the Fourth Ruler and presented You to her.**

“I appeal to you, and I tell that neither me nor my crew would ever abuse the
power of the Nine Rulers or their Keys, which I swear on the lifeblood of these
lands and on the Curators of Twil through which I have housed.”
